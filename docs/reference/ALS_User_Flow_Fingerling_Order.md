# ALS User Flow: First-Time Fingerling Order (KB-2026-012)

> Note (28 Nov 2026): written for an earlier scope that included payment proof upload, tracking and receipts. Those steps (12 to 16) are OUT of the agreed scope. The current scope ends at the WhatsApp handoff with a saved order request. Keep the lessons on the WhatsApp handoff and sold-out fish.

KoomBei Digital | Working document for Week 1 (plan and design) | November 2026

## Who and what
A first-time customer, most likely a woman farmer on a low-cost Android phone, recommended to ALS by someone she trusts. Task: order fingerlings.

## The flow
Shapes: (action) [screen] <decision>

1. (Opens a link a friend shared on WhatsApp)
2. [Fingerlings page: sizes, prices, availability]
3. (Chooses a size)
4. <Available?> No: [Next batch date, notify me when ready]. Yes: continue
5. (Sets quantity and collection or delivery date)
6. <Large farm order?> Yes: [Request a quote]. No: continue
7. [Order summary: delivery or farm pickup, total]
8. (Adds name and phone number; no account needed)
9. <All correct?> No: back to the order summary. Yes: continue
10. (Taps Send order on WhatsApp); order is saved to the dashboard at this moment
11. <WhatsApp opened?> No: [Copy order text or call ALS; order already saved]. Yes: done
12. to 16. (Earlier scope, not in this phase: payment details, proof upload, tracking page, receipt.)

## Lessons
- **Start screen:** the fingerlings page, not the homepage, because ALS grows by recommendation and people share product links. That page must stand on its own: one line on who ALS is and its 17 years, real photos, availability and the WhatsApp button.
- **The WhatsApp handoff is the riskiest moment.** Show the order reference on screen before WhatsApp opens.
- **WhatsApp may not open,** for example a buyer abroad on a laptop. Offer "copy order" and a call button. The order is already saved either way.
- **Sold-out live fish.** "Next batch ready on [date]" plus notify me is more useful than a plain "out of stock".
